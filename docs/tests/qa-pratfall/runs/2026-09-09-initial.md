## Root failure and repair addendum — 2026-09-09

An independent replay after this record exposed a race in the Q17 harness observation. The full
command exited 1 at the compound stderr-limit assertion and produced no result JSON. Three focused
reproductions observed pending as `false`, `false`, and `true`; every attempt still returned 1 with
`stderr_limit_exceeded` and released the descendant lock. The product behavior passed, but the
harness could miss the pending state because its fake began flooding immediately after `READY`.

The original JSON and all evidence above remain unchanged. The harness now uses a deterministic
`READY`/`GO` exchange, bounded invocation and cleanup deadlines, complete public-result assertions,
and direct source/archive/installed runtime manifests. The repaired full installed run passed all
17 offline scenarios. See the [repair record](2026-09-09-repair.md) and its
[retest evidence](2026-09-09-repair-retest.json).
